# Kick-off Meeting – CoTEDI Project Begins in the Netherlands

**Date: 28 February 2024**

The Erasmus+ CoTEDI project officially started with a three-day kick-off meeting held in the Netherlands, bringing together all partner institutions to align goals, share visions, and plan the path ahead.

During the sessions, representatives from each organization presented their contributions, discussed coordination strategies, and explored the project’s shared mission: to foster computational thinking through inclusive and diverse educational approaches. The meeting combined formal presentations with hands-on moments, reinforcing collaboration and building the foundations for successful teamwork.

This event marked the first step in a three-year journey to co-design, test, and disseminate meaningful educational resources that empower both teachers and students in diverse classroom settings.
