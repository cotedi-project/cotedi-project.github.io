  Join the CoTEDI Team at our third and final Multiplier Event in Lisbon, Portugal to learn about using inclusive Robotics in the classroom. This event is a half day event for teachers, educational practitioners and researchers is held at Instituto Superior Técnico (Alameda Campus), this Multiplier Event brings together teachers and educators from early childhood to secondary school to explore computational thinking through inclusive and innovative practices. The morning program includes a keynote on computational thinking in inclusive education, a presentation of the CoTEDI project and its results, and hands-on workshops on educational robotics, unplugged activities and game-based learning tools. Join the CoTEDI Team at our third and final Multiplier Event at the [Instituto Superior Técnico in Lisbon, Portugal](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zQsQ8qi8nevQhjLRA). This event allows you to find out about the project&#39;s outcomes and findings. The event allows to explore how to bring robots into your teaching and focus on different facets of inclusion in the classroom. 

The program is jam packed with inspirational talks, hands-on workshops and design activities to create your own teaching experiences. 

The preliminary program is as follows: 

**09.00 – 09.15**: Walk-in and registration
**09.15 – 09.30**: Welcome &amp; opening
**09.30 – 10.00**: Keynote / inspirational talks: Computational thinking in inclusive education 
**10.00 – 10.30**: CoTEDI Project objectives, methods and results 
**10.30 – 11.30**: Hands-on workshops / demonstrations
**11.30 – 11.45**: Short break
**11.45 – 12.30**: Co-Design workshop to build your own classroom
**12.30 – 12.50**: Plenary discussion &amp; feedback
**12.50 – 13.00**: Wrap-up &amp; closing

The event is free of charge and registration takes place on site.
