description: As part of the CoTEDI project, we offer training sessions for teachers to introduce the concept of computational thinking and explore how it can be developed in the classroom. During these sessions, we present practical strategies and activities that can be used with students, as well as tools and guidelines for assessing the development of computational thinking. The goal is to empower educators to confidently implement and adapt these activities in their own teaching contexts.
