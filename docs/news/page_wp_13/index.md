<h1>Integrating Programmable Robots to Foster<br />Computational Thinking in Early Childhood<br />Classrooms</h1>
<p><strong>This work is co-funded by the Erasmus+ project CoTEDI, which is also co-financed by the European Union under the call-key action 2023-1-NL01-KA220-SCH-000152037 – OID E10207981</strong></p>
<p>Gamarra-Expósito, A., Martín-Barroso, E., &amp; Zapata-Cáceres, M. (2025). Integrating Programmable Robots to Foster Computational Thinking in Early Childhood Classrooms. <i>Proc. Special Issue on Innovations and Applications of Educational Robots and Robotics (EduRobotX SIG, EATEL), CEUR-WS EduRobotX</i>.</p>
<p><strong>Abstract.</strong> Computational thinking and educational robotics are becoming key competencies for creating<br />competent digital citizens in today&#8217;s world. The development of these skills has been gradually<br />implemented in Primary and Secondary Education, but there is still a long way to go, especially in<br />their use in Early Childhood Education. The use of these technologies from an early age has shown to<br />have positive effects on students&#8217; education. This paper presents an intervention among 3-year-old<br />students using the Bee-Bot robot. The study includes both unplugged activities and activities with the<br />robot to develop computational thinking skills. The results show an improvement in the acquisition of<br />these concepts with meaningful learning after conducting the robotics sessions. Additionally, the<br />obtained results are analysed and options for their improvement are discussed. The difficulties and<br />limitations of this study are also addressed.</p>
